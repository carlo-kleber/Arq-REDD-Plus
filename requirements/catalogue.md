# Requirements catalogue

Based on Table 4 of the revised manuscript. Acceptance criteria are proposed, not experimentally verified. Source labels follow the manuscript bibliography; exact bibliographic details should be checked against that bibliography.

| ID | Requirement | Source | Stakeholders | Layers | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TC1 | Versioning of project documentation, emission baselines, additionality evidence, and methodologies | Verra 2025d,c | PP, AU | A | The applicable documentation, additionality evidence, and methodology versions can be identified and their revision history retrieved. |
| TC2 | Traceability of MRV evidence and validation and verification decisions | Marke et al. 2022; Verra 2025d | PP, AU | N,A | Recorded assessments can be associated with supporting evidence, responsible entities, and applicable methodology. |
| TC3 | Management of carbon credit life-cycle states | Kotsialou et al. 2021; Verra 2025b | PP, AU, RG, BY | C,A | Issuance, transfer, retirement and applicable cancellation follow authorized state transitions. |
| TC4 | Recording of reversals and associated accounting adjustments | Verra 2025d,a | PP, AU, RG | C,A | Reported reversals and applicable adjustments can be recorded and traced to affected records. |
| TC5 | Prevention of duplicate issuance and repeated credit use within the system | Verra 2025d,b | PP, RG, BY | C,A | Duplicate issuance and attempts to reuse retired credits are rejected. |
| GP1 | Stakeholder identification and role-based authorization | Howson et al. 2019; Marke et al. 2022 | ALL | N,A | Operations are associated with identifiable actors or authorized credentials; unauthorized requests are rejected. |
| GP2 | Protection of sensitive information | Howson et al. 2019; Marke et al. 2022 | ALL | N,A | Access is restricted according to authorization and visibility policies. |
| GP3 | Traceability of socio-environmental safeguards, benefit-sharing arrangements, and grievance resolution | Kotsialou et al. 2021; Verra 2025d | PP, LC, AU | N,A | Applicable consultation, consent, benefit-sharing, and grievance resolution records can be retrieved by authorized stakeholders. |
| CI1 | Agreement on valid distributed ledger state | Xu et al. 2017; Belchior et al. 2021 | NP | C | Consensus establishes an agreed ledger state under specified operating and fault assumptions. |
| CI2 | Integrity and auditability of recorded information | Howson et al. 2019; Marke et al. 2022 | ALL | C,A | Unauthorized alterations can be detected and authorized corrections preserve an auditable history. |
| IT1 | Integration with external MRV systems and carbon registries | Marke et al. 2022; Belchior et al. 2021 | PP, AU, RG | N,A | External information can be exchanged through defined interfaces with identifiable sources and validation procedures. |
| IT2 | Reconciliation with external registries and jurisdictional accounting systems | Verra 2024, 2025b; UNFCCC 2021 | PP, RG, AU | A | Applicable external identifiers, accounting records and authorization information can be associated and reconciled, with discrepancies identified. |
| IT3 | Compatibility among technological decisions and preservation of architectural responsibilities | Xu et al. 2017; Belchior et al. 2021 | SD, NP | N,C,A | Alternative configurations preserve responsibilities and interface contracts and satisfy applicable compatibility constraints. |

PP: project proponents; AU: auditing entities; RG: registry operators and relevant regulatory authorities; BY: carbon credit buyers; LC: local communities and Indigenous Peoples; NP: network participants; SD: system designers and operators; ALL: all relevant stakeholders. N: Network; C: Consensus; A: Application.
