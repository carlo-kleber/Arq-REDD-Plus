# Interoperability and registry reconciliation

Syntactic interoperability concerns compatible data structures; semantic interoperability concerns consistent interpretation; operational interoperability concerns coordination of operations and state transitions across independent systems.

A6, A7 and IF10 support these responsibilities. Exchanged records must consistently identify projects, credits, quantities, life-cycle states, methodology references and applicable institutional authorizations. Identify authoritative external records, detect discrepancies and record reconciliation outcomes. Communication failures and unresolved discrepancies must not be treated as successful reconciliation.

Gateways and cross-chain protocols are possible implementation choices. Transferring a token does not necessarily transfer the corresponding carbon credit or rights. Preserve credit identification, quantity conservation, authorization and consistency with authoritative registry records.

No particular standard or cross-chain mechanism is prescribed. Effective interoperability depends on the selected technologies, external systems and institutional arrangements. Requirements: TC3–TC5, IT1–IT3.
