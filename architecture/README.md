# Arq-REDD+ Architectural Specification

Arq-REDD+ is a domain-specific, technology-agnostic reference architecture for carbon credit management in Blockchain-based REDD+ projects.

## Scope

The architecture separates domain-specific functional responsibilities from technological implementation decisions. Technological agnosticism refers to design-time flexibility subject to explicit compatibility constraints, rather than seamless post-deployment substitution.

## Specification overview

The architectural specification comprises:

- 13 requirements and their traceability relationships;
- 15 functional components organized into the Network, Consensus, and Application Layers;
- 10 logical interface contracts;
- 10 technological decision points and associated compatibility constraints;
- two contrasting candidate technological configurations;
- eight architectural evaluation scenarios.

The specification also addresses the carbon credit life cycle, external MRV evidence, registry reconciliation, governance, security, and stakeholder inclusion.

## Evaluation scope

The architectural evaluation examines structural coherence, requirement coverage, and expected behavior under specified scenarios. It does not establish conformance or interoperability in deployed systems. The complementary simulation-based evaluation concerns the performance and scalability of a particular technological instantiation, not intrinsic performance properties of the reference architecture.

## Related research

This documentation accompanies the manuscript *Arq-REDD+: An Agnostic Architectural Model for Carbon Credit Management in Blockchain-Based REDD+ Projects*, by Marcela Ceschim Caburlão and Carlo Kleber da Silva Rodrigues (Universidade Federal do ABC).
